The values Part 13 leaves to the design

Part 13 states requirements and refuses to name values, so that no value is written twice. This file names them. Where Part 13 says “the design work”, it means this file. Read it beside 13-design-system.md; every section below is numbered to the clause it satisfies.

13.1–13.3

13.2

Modifier chips

Modifiers sit after the state badge and may collapse into the row’s plain-text expiry line on narrow viewports (D‑03). They are outlined, never solid, so they never compete with the shared badge beside them.

Expires in 12 hours Password Expired In trash

Warm outline is reserved for the expiring modifier, matching shared’s ink — that is the whole of the warm family’s licence (13.3). Expired is struck through so it reads as expired without its colour. In-trash is dashed so it reads as provisional.

13.4

Identifiers

Monospace, selectable, always paired with a copy control, never truncated by CSS. Where a long identifier must fit, it wraps or scrolls; it does not ellipsize, because a truncated hash is a hash you cannot use.

Advisories, since they may not be warm

Warning and advisory banners are neutral: paper ground, #201e1d text, a 3px #201e1d left edge, and the word “Note” or “Warning” set in the serif’s italic. Component anatomy

Buttons

Destructive is outlined and fills on hover, so revoking is one click without ever being the loudest thing on a screen. Widening access is the solid primary; narrowing it is the outline (13.1, and START-HERE’s second point).

Focus — 13.9

2px ink ring at 2px offset. Ink rather than cyan, so the ring clears 3:1 against a cyan button, a magenta badge and paper alike — one ring that works against every element in the product. On dark, #ece9e7.

13.8

The recipient pages are elsewhere

R1–R7 cannot use anything on this page — no webfont, no external asset, no dashboard chrome, and a style block that is byte-identical across every one of them. They are therefore not designed here. They ship as ten complete HTML documents covering the seven pages (R1 has three states, R2 has two) in recipient/, ready to serve as-is, with the block’s constraints explained in recipient/README.md.
